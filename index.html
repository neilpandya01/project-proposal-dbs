Project proposal

Wingmates

Your flights, your spots, your crew.

Avgeeks juggle separate apps for flight logs, tracking, spotting, and news. Wingmates is a free social app for avgeeks, built by an avgeek: log the flights you take and the planes you spot, follow friends, and see them live on the map while they fly. See user-reported and current aviation news. All in one place, on any phone.

Flight log Live friends map Spotting Aviation news Android + iPhone Free

The problem

Aviation apps are each good at one piece of the hobby. Avgeeks end up using one app to log flights, another to track them, another to spot planes, plus news sites and general social media to share and keep up.

NeedTodayWhat's missing
Flight log and statsFlighty, Jumpseat, myFlightradar24Flighty is Apple-only; importing flights older than a year needs Pro ($59.99/yr). Jumpseat is iPhone-only.
Friends' flights and statsFlighty Friends, JumpseatCan't open a friend's profile to see their map, stats, or shared routes.
Spotting logSkycards, Aviatter, spreadsheetsKept separate from your flights and from live data. Friends features feel shallow.
Aviation newsNews sites, Reddit, XScattered, and not tied to the airports, airlines, or flights you care about.

The gap: no free app connects flights you take, planes you spot, your friends, and the news around them, on both Android and iPhone. Wingmates competes on integration, not on features that are missing everywhere else.

Strongest needs

Richer friend profiles and stats, Android support, spotting tied to live data, and easier flight history.

Counterpoint

Some users prefer minimal social features, and parents worry about kids and chat. So social is optional, private by default, and there are no DMs.

How it works

Next.js on Vercel and Supabase (database, storage, realtime, scheduled jobs). It's an installable web app, so it works on Android and iPhone for $0. Supabase Realtime moves planes on friends' maps live, and its scheduled jobs handle tracking and news.

CLIs and MCP servers: Supabase CLI + MCP (database changes, scheduled jobs, checking stored flights), Vercel CLI (deploys and logs), GitHub CLI (repo and pull requests), Playwright MCP (test the map, feed, and profile in a real browser).

Live map

Every minute, a job checks flights that should be in the air, looks them up on adsb.fi, and pushes positions to friends' maps.

News

Every 15 minutes, a job pulls RSS feeds and FAA alerts into one feed next to user reports. Filter by your airports and airlines.

Adding a flight

"UA 1234, Nov 20" → route from BTS data → user confirms → saved.

    Publish proposal. Set up Supabase and Vercel.

  2. Nov 10–17 · v1

    Profiles, flights, friends, live map, news feed, basic user reports.

  3. Nov 17–24

    Spotting, stats on friend profiles.

  4. Nov 24 → Dec 1 · v2

    Comparisons, achievements, notifications, trending reports. Polish for the fair.

Focus: Holistic Journey

Sign up→Add a flight→Add a friend→See them on the map→Read and post news

I chose it because the app only works if every step of that path works on the live site, and it doesn't need AI.
